ॐ श्री लक्ष्मी नारायणाय नमः
Om Shree Lakshmi Narayanaya Namah
Meaning —

This sacred mantra is an offering to Lord Vishnu (Narayan) and Goddess Lakshmi. "Om" is the universal sound; chanting this mantra invokes blessings of prosperity, wealth, harmony and protection in life.
